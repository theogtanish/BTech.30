Lenovo IdeaPad Gaming 3 (15ACH6)

The Lenovo IdeaPad Gaming 3 15ACH6 is a 15.6-inch budget gaming laptop featuring a Full HD (1920x1080) IPS display with a 120Hz refresh rate and anti-glare coating. It is powered by an AMD Ryzen 5 5500H processor and equipped with NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050, providing solid performance for esports and moderate AAA gaming.

Key specifications include:

  1. Display: 15.6" FHD IPS, 120Hz, 250 nits brightness
  2. Graphics: NVIDIA RTX 2050 (4GB GDDR6 VRAM)
  3. Connectivity: Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0/5.1, Gigabit Ethernet, USB-C, HDMI 2.0.
  4. Build: Shadow Black chassis, weighing approximately 2.25 kg.
  5. Thermals: Enhanced cooling system with dual air outlets and heat pipes to manage heat during gaming.
